# Plan 07b: IO, ADC and the Reload button

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:**
- DI1/DI2 (active low) and DO1/DO2 (through the 74HC573) work as on the ESP32: debounce, invert, DO modes, six timers, `keepOutputs`.
- AI1/AI2 come from ADC3 with hardware oversampling, triggered by TIM6, through BDMA in two halves.
  - Each published value is the mean of the last complete 100 ms window.
  - The two-point calibration comes from the factory record.
  - Faults recover by themselves.
- Reload held 3–15 s while the app runs restores the factory configuration.
- `GET`/`POST /api/io` replace the 503.

**Spec:**
- §3: the pins.
- §5.2: SPI4 0x3F0000, the factory record.
- §6.1: tasks `io` (prio 6, 3 KB) and `reload` (prio 3, 2 KB).
- §6.3: init order, `iomgr` after `netmgr` and before `serial`.
- §6.6: the ADC driver.
- §9.1: configs read at boot.
- §10.2: `/api/io`.
- §11.4: Reload.

**Roadmap:** row 07, the half not done in 07a. The production factory-record loader and the full ADC acceptance (P1) stay in Plan 11.

## Rules that apply

- **Configs are read once at boot:** `io`, plus `system` for the timezone. A change applies after a restart, so there is no `store_subscribe`.
- Keep it simple, and run only the affected tests. Codex reviews before the PR.
- **The behavioural baseline is the ESP32 `iomgr.c`:**
  - DI debounce (`diDebounce` ms) and `diInvert`;
  - DO modes 0 (follow), 1 ("output hold": a pulse of `do<i>FlipDelay` s, then back to rest) and 2 ("timer flip": toggles every `do<i>FlipDelay` s while commanded on);
  - `keepOutputs`, which saves the *commanded* mask: a pulse as its rest state, a blinking output as on;
  - six time-of-day timers (`timer<i>Enabled`, `Action` 0 = restart and 1 = set an output, `Output`, `OutputAction` 0 open, 1 close, 2 toggle, `Time` "HH:MM[:SS]");
  - the timers fire for every second in (last check, now], and nothing fires after a clock jump of more than 120 s or while the clock is not set;
  - `GET /api/io` returns `{"do":[..],"di":[..],"ai":[µA..],"ao":[]}`, and `POST` takes `{"do": i, "value": bool}`.
- **The ESP32's Reload task is the Reload baseline:** a 50 ms poll; on release after 3–15 s, a factory reset, then a restart. Added by §11.4: a button already held when the app starts counts only from the next press.

## Architecture

### `components/iomgr/io_core.{h,c}`

Pure C, host-tested (`test_io_core`). It holds all the state of the IO, and takes time as an argument (`now_ms`, and UTC seconds with the timezone).
- `io_core_init(c, cfg)`, where `cfg` is `io_cfg_t`, parsed from the `io` JSON by `io_cfg_parse()` (also pure).
- **DI:** `io_core_di(c, i, pin_level, now_ms)`. Active low: active means `!pin`, and `diInvert` flips it. A new level must hold `diDebounce` ms.
- **DO:** `io_core_do_set(c, i, on, now_ms)` applies the mode, and `io_core_tick(c, now_ms)` ends pulses and toggles blinks at their deadlines.
  - The outputs are a 2-bit mask, `io_core_outputs(c)`.
  - `io_core_commanded(c)` is the mask that `keepOutputs` saves.
- **Timers:** `bool io_core_timers(c, utc_s, tz_hours, clock_set, now_ms)` applies the output actions inside the core and returns true when a timer asks for a restart. There is no callback, so nothing re-enters the caller's lock (Codex r1).
- **`keepOutputs` persistence:**
  - every change of the commanded mask sets `dirty`;
  - `bool io_core_persist_take(c, &mask)` returns the *latest* commanded mask, and clears `dirty`, when it differs from the saved one;
  - `io_core_persist_done(c, mask, ok)` records the save, or sets `dirty` again when it failed.

  With one writer reading the latest mask, saves can never land out of order (Codex r1).
- **`keepOutputs` at boot:** `io_core_restore(c, mask, now_ms)`. Outputs in blink mode resume blinking.

### `components/iomgr/adc_core.{h,c}`

Pure C, host-tested (`test_adc_core`). It covers §12.1 "ADC":
- **Half handoff:** `adc_core_half(a, const uint16_t half[100][2], seq_start, seq_read, ctx)`.
  - It sums the 100 pairs into `uint32_t`, per channel.
  - It then calls `seq_read(ctx)` (on the board, a read of the ISR's `seq`): when that differs from `seq_start`, read before the sum, the half may have been overwritten. The window is dropped and `late` is counted (Codex r1).
  - Else the window's mean is published.
- **Faults:** `adc_core_fail(a)` marks both channels failed. A value is published again only after a full new half, at start and after a recovery.
- **Conversion:** `adc_core_ua(a, ch)`. First the nominal current, `I = mean × 3.0 / (65535 × 0.499 × 0.120)` mA. Then the channel's two-point calibration, `I' = gain × I + offset`. The result is in µA, 0 when failed, with `adc_core_ok(a, ch)` beside it.
- **Factory record:** `frec_parse(bytes, len, &rec)` and `frec_encode(&rec, out)`, then `frec_choose(a_ok, a, b_ok, b, &cal)`:
  - the first valid copy wins;
  - with none, the nominal coefficients (gain 1, offset 0) are used, with `calibrated = false`.
- **The record layout** (little endian, 64 bytes, one per 4 KB sector at SPI4 0x3F0000 and 0x3F1000):

  | Offset | Content |
  |---|---|
  | 0 | magic `"SBFR"` |
  | 4 | version 1 (u16), length 64 (u16) |
  | 8 | `gain[2]`, `offset_ma[2]` (float32) |
  | 24 | reserved, 0xFF up to 60 |
  | 60 | CRC-32 of bytes 0..59 (`crc32.h`) |

  A gain outside 0.8–1.25 or an offset outside ±1 mA counts as invalid.

### `components/iomgr/adc_drv.c`

Board only. It follows the §6.6 table:
- **ADC3:** INP0 and INP1, 16 bit, ranks 1 and 2, 64.5 cycles.
  - Hardware oversampling ×16 with a right shift of 4, all 16 conversions from one trigger.
  - Triggered by TIM6_TRGO at 1 kHz (TIM6: prescaler 239, period 999 at 240 MHz).
  - `DMNGT` = circular.
  - The clock is PLL2P 20 MHz, already in `HAL_ADC_MspInit`, which HAL divides by 2 on rev V: an analog clock of 10 MHz, `BOOST` = 01. The bench reads `BOOST`, `OVSR` and `OVSS` back.
- **HAL:** `HAL_ADC_Init`, the channel setup and `HAL_ADCEx_Calibration_Start(OFFSET_LINEARITY, SINGLE_ENDED)`, whose return code is checked. HAL does nothing else.
- **A failed init or calibration** leaves both channels failed for the whole run, logged once.
- **The fallback:** if the bench's cross-talk check fails, the only allowed fallback is 387.5 cycles with oversampling ×8 (`OVSR` 7, `OVSS` 3), a 634 µs scan. Every combination's scan time must stay under 900 µs; `adc_drv.c` states it in a `_Static_assert` on the computed time.
- **BDMA channel 0** (DMAMUX2 request 17), half-words, circular, into `adc_buf[2][100][2]` in `.adc_buf` (0x3800FC00, MPU region 3).
- **Interrupts:** BDMA HT, TC and TE, plus the ADC's `OVR`, all in ITCM. Each HT or TC writes `ready_half`, increments `seq`, and notifies the `io` task; nothing happens per sample.
- **Recovery** runs in `io`, in the order §6.6 gives:
  1. stop TIM6;
  2. `ADSTP`, and the BDMA off;
  3. clear the flags;
  4. reload the address, the count and the ranks;
  5. the BDMA on, then `ADSTART`;
  6. TIM6 on.

  There is no deep-power-down, so the calibration stays.
- **Counters:** `irqs`, `errors`, `recoveries`, and `late` (from `adc_core`).
- **Bench hook:** `adc_drv_inject_fault()` disables the BDMA channel, so the next conversion overruns (`OVR`).

### `components/iomgr/iomgr.c` and `include/iomgr.h`

Board side.
- **API:** `iomgr_start(io_cfg_json, sys_json)`, `iomgr_do_set(i, on)`, `iomgr_do_get`, `iomgr_di_get`, `iomgr_ai_ua`, `iomgr_ai_ok`, `iomgr_state_json()`, `iomgr_status(io_status_t *)`.
- **The task `io`** (prio 6, 3 KB) waits for a notification for at most 10 ms. Each wake-up:
  - handles an ADC half (`adc_core_half`) or a fault (recovery);
  - samples DI1 (PB7) and DI2 (PB6);
  - runs `io_core_tick`;
  - checks the timers every 200 ms.

  The work per wake-up is bounded (P2).
- **DO writes go through the 74HC573:** set D1 (PD10) and D2 (PD11), then a pulse on LE (PD14). `board_latch_zero()` has already enabled OE.
- **Locking:** a mutex guards `io_core`, because `iomgr_do_set` is called from the web task (later also MQTT and linkage). It is held only around the core calls.
- **`keepOutputs`:**
  - The `io` task and `iomgr_do_set` never touch the flash: they only mark the mask `dirty`.
  - The `reload` task's 50 ms poll is the one writer. Under the mutex it takes the latest mask (`io_core_persist_take`); outside it, it calls `store_state_set("do", mask)`; then `io_core_persist_done`.
  - A slow FlashDB write (430 ms seen in `store_check`) delays only the save, never DI, the DO timing or the ADC (ruling 3).
  - At boot, `store_state_get("do")` feeds `io_core_restore`.
- **Timers** are checked on the `io` task under the mutex, through `io_core_timers`. A restart is asked for after the mutex is released (`sysmgr_restart_later(1000)`).
- **The latch:** after each core call, `iomgr` writes the outputs to the 74HC573 if `io_core_outputs` changed.

### `components/sysmgr/reload.{h,c}`

Pure C, host-tested (`test_reload`).
- `reload_step(r, pressed, now_ms)` returns `RELOAD_NONE` or `RELOAD_FACTORY`:
  - 3000–15000 ms held, then released: factory;
  - shorter or longer: nothing;
  - pressed at the first step: ignored until a release.
- **The task `reload`** (prio 3, 2 KB) polls PA8 (active low) every 50 ms, and saves `keepOutputs` (see `iomgr`).
  - At 3 s it logs "release now to restore the factory settings".
  - On factory, it calls `store_factory_reset()` (the log is kept) and `sysmgr_restart_later(1500)`.

### Web, status and bench console

- **`api.c`:**
  - `GET /api/io` gives `iomgr_state_json()`;
  - `POST /api/io` takes `{"do": i, "value": bool}`; `"ao"` gives 400 ("no analog outputs"), and a bad index 400;
  - `/api/status` gets `aiCalibrated`, and `mem.adc` = {`irqs`, `late`, `errors`, `recoveries`}.
- **The bench console** (`app/bench_io.c`):
  - `a`: the raw window means, the µA values, `ok`, the counters and the ADC registers (`BOOST`, `OVSR`, `OVSS`, `SMPR`);
  - `A <g1> <o1> <g2> <o2>`: writes the factory record (both copies), the bench calibration;
  - `J`: injects an ADC fault.

## Rulings to approve

1. **The DO pulses and blinks run on the `io` task's 10 ms tick,** not on FreeRTOS software timers. §4 asks for software timers whose callback only notifies; the tick is the same thing with one task fewer. It is also host-testable, because `io_core` takes `now_ms`. Cost if wrong: a pulse is 0–10 ms late.
2. **The factory record is written in 07b only by the bench console `A`,** from the values `io_check.py` computes. The SWD production loader stays in Plan 11 (roadmap P3). Cost if wrong: none for production; the loader writes the same 64-byte layout.
3. **`keepOutputs` is saved by the `reload` task,** the one low-priority periodic task, as the only writer, and always with the latest mask. The ESP32 wrote on the caller's task. Cost if wrong: a save happens up to 50 ms after the change (plus the FlashDB time); a restart in that window restores the previous mask.
4. **The Modbus IO slave (`iomgr_remote`, `io.modbusAddress`) moves to Plan 08,** with the Modbus gateway that routes requests to it. It reuses `iomgr_di_get`, `iomgr_do_get`, `iomgr_ai_ua` and `iomgr_ai_ok`.
5. **The bench checks the ADC with one 4–20 mA source** (the equipment on the bench), with AI1 and AI2 in series for calibration and accuracy. These stay in Plan 11 (P1), with a reference meter and an injector:
   - the 50/60 Hz noise rejection;
   - the absolute accuracy;
   - cross-talk against a second held source.

   07b measures cross-talk with AI2 open: it must stay within ±0.01 mA of its open reading while AI1 steps 4 → 20 mA.

## Bench (`test/bench/io_check.py`)

**Wiring:**
- DO1's relay contact drives DI1, and DO2's drives DI2, through the board's supply for the DI optos;
- the 4–20 mA source drives AI1 and AI2 in series;
- the Reload button is pressed by hand when the script asks.

1. **DI/DO:**
   - `POST /api/io` DO1 on: `/api/io` shows DO1 on and DI1 active within debounce + 100 ms; off likewise; the same for DO2.
   - `diInvert` 1 inverts.
   - **Mode 1** (pulse 2 s): DO1 on, then back off after 2 s ± 0.1 s, seen on DI1.
   - **Mode 2** (flip 1 s): DI1 toggles about every 1 s while commanded on, and stays off after an off.
   - **`keepOutputs`:** DO1 on, restart, DO1 still on. With `keepOutputs` 0, off after the restart.
   - **A timer** set one minute ahead (the clock set by `POST /api/time`) closes DO2 at that minute.
   - **The bootloader:** with `keepOutputs` 0, DO1 on and then a restart leaves DO1 off (the bootloader latched zeros). The relay releasing *during* the reset is not visible to the board's own DI; it is watched by hand (the relay's LED and click) once.
2. **ADC**, with the source at 4, 12 and 20 mA:
   - **Calibration:** the raw means at 4 and 20 mA give `gain` and `offset` per channel; `A` writes them, and after a restart `aiCalibrated` is true.
   - 12 mA reads 12.000 ± 0.05 mA on both channels, against the source.
   - The standard deviation over 60 s is ≤ 0.005 mA.
   - **Latency:** a step 4 → 20 mA shows in `/api/io` in ≤ 700 ms. The script polls every 50 ms after the source is switched by hand, so the measurement takes the board's own timestamps.
   - **Recovery:** after `J`, no value is published, then a value is published again within 300 ms.
   - **IRQs:** the ADC and BDMA interrupts are ≤ 10 a second, from `mem.adc.irqs` over 10 s.
   - **Registers:** `BOOST` = 01, `OVSR` = 15, `OVSS` = 4.
   - **Cross-talk** (ruling 5).
3. **Reload**, pressed by hand when asked:
   - 1 s: nothing;
   - 5 s: the factory configuration (a marker config is gone; the log is kept);
   - 20 s: nothing;
   - held while the board restarts: nothing until released, then 5 s does the factory reset.

**Regressions:** `serial_check.py` (`io` sits before `serial` in init), `web_check.py` (`/api/io` is no longer 503: its case changes), `store_check.py`.

## Review Focus

1. **A `diDebounce` shorter than the 10 ms tick:** it acts as one tick (10 ms), never as zero.
2. **A DO command in mode 1 during a pulse:** commanding the rest state ends the pulse; commanding the pulsed state again restarts its time (the ESP32's rule). `test_io_core` pins both.
3. **A timer at "23:59:59" across midnight, and the clock set while timers are enabled:** "23:59:59" fires once; a jump of more than 120 s fires nothing. `test_io_core` pins both.
4. **A factory record with a good CRC but absurd coefficients** (gain 0 or 1e9): rejected, so the nominal ones are used. `test_adc_core` pins it.
5. **A fault during the very first window, before any value:** nothing is published until a full half arrives after the recovery. `test_adc_core` pins it.

## Codex round 1 (NOT APPROVED), and the fixes

1. **The ADC handoff** read `seq` before the sum only. `seq_read` is now called after the sum, and the test moves `seq` during it.
2. **`keepOutputs` saves could land out of order** (two callers). There is now one writer, the `reload` task, and it takes the latest mask under the mutex. A test covers a change during a save.
3. **Rulings 1 and 3 conflicted:** a FlashDB write on `io` would have delayed DI and DO. The `io` task never writes the flash now.
4. **A timer callback re-entering the mutex:** `io_core_timers` applies outputs inside the core and returns only a restart request, acted on after the mutex is released.
