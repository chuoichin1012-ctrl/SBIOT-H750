# Plan 10: LTE (EC600G-EU, CMUX, PPPoS, link priority)

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** the gateway reaches the network over the EC600G-EU as well as over Ethernet, following `network.priority`.

**The module (the user, 2026-10-16):**
- The board carries an **EC600G-EU**, not the EC600U that spec §7.2 was written for.
- The user's call: use the EC600U's timings (PWRKEY 2.5 s, `RESET_N` 200 ms, the AT deadlines), and accept them if the module works with them on the bench (ruling 13).
- **No SIM is fitted yet,** so everything that needs one is a bench step for later (ruling 12).
- **PPP runs on CMUX DLC1,** and an AT channel (DLC2) keeps the signal readable while PPP is up.
- **The power sequence never powers off a running module.** It tracks the module as ON, OFF or UNKNOWN.
- **The default route and the DNS follow the preferred link.** Connections bound to the old link are closed, so the services dial again over the new one.

**Architecture:**
- **Pure C, host-tested:**
  - `proto/cmux.c`: the 27.010 basic-mode codec and state machine, pull-based for TX;
  - `proto/at_parse.c`: AT replies;
  - `netmgr/lte_power.c`: the power sequence and the recovery decisions, with the hardware as callbacks;
  - `netmgr/netcfg.c`: the route choice.
- **The board side:**
  - **`lte_uart.c`:** UART3 with circular RX DMA (4 KB, HT/TC/IDLE) and DMA TX.
  - **`cmux_rx` task** (prio 25, 2 KB): the only reader of the ring and the only writer to UART3.
    - It feeds the CMUX parser and hands DLC1 bytes to `pppos_input_tcpip` (`ERR_MEM` retried).
    - DLC2 bytes go into the AT stream buffer.
    - It pulls frames from `cmux_tx_next` to send.
  - **`lte` task** (prio 5, 6 KB): the power sequence, AT, CMUX open, the dial, PPP, signal polling and recovery.
  - **`netmgr`:** adds the PPP netif and chooses the default route.

**Tech stack:** lwIP 2.2.1 PPPoS (vendored `src/netif/ppp`, PAP and CHAP with lwIP's built-in MD5), FreeRTOS, LL DMA and USART3. On the bench: the EC600G-EU; later, a data SIM and a public broker reached over LTE; the RS485 adapter on UART1.

**Spec:** [2026-10-14-stm32-firmware-design.md](../specs/2026-10-14-stm32-firmware-design.md):
- §7.2: LTE;
- §5.3 and §5.4: buffers by region;
- §6.1: tasks `cmux_rx` 2 KB at prio 25 and `lte` 6 KB at prio 5;
- §12.1: the CMUX host-test row;

- §4: init order (UART3 is `netmgr/lte`'s, not `serial`'s).

**Roadmap:** row 10, "`proto/cmux.c` state machine and flow control, `cmux_rx`, PPPoS (`ERR_MEM` retry, TX callback returns 0), EC600U power sequence with ON/OFF/UNKNOWN, link priority". Its gates:
- Modem ON/OFF/UNKNOWN and the 2.5 s PWRKEY pulse;

- a PPP frame cut between callback calls.

## Global Constraints

- **Module timing** (spec §7.2's EC600U table, used for the EC600G-EU, ruling 13):
  - PWRKEY pressed for **2.5 s** to power on, and **never 3 s or longer**;
  - `RESET_N` for **200 ms**;
  - "UART ready" means `AT` answers, tried every 500 ms, with a **15 s** deadline from the release;
  - powered off only on the URC `POWERED DOWN` after `AT+QPOWD=1`, with a 60 s deadline. A silent UART is never taken for "off".
- **The start sequence:**
  1. `AT` for 3 s;
  2. then `RESET_N` and wait;
  3. then PWRKEY 2.5 s and wait;
  4. then `MODEM NOT RESPONDING`, 60 s, and again from step 2.

  From OFF (confirmed), PWRKEY alone.
- **No SIM** (`AT+CPIN?` is not `READY`, or it gets `+CME ERROR: 10`):
  - the state is `NO SIM`, and nothing is dialled;
  - `AT+CPIN?` is asked again every 60 s;
  - while the module itself answers, there is no `RESET_N`, no CFUN and no RECOVER.
- **The CMUX command:** `AT+CMUX=0,0,5,2048,20,3,30,10,2`. N1 is 2048, T1 200 ms, N2 3. The 2-byte length field is supported. The RX frame buffer holds 2048 + 7 bytes or more (2112 B).
- **Channels:** DLC0 is control, DLC1 PPP, DLC2 AT (`AT+CSQ`, `AT+QNWINFO` every 20 s).
- **Flow control:**
  - FCoff/FCon stop and resume every data DLC, and DLC0 keeps working;
  - MSC with FC stops or resumes exactly the DLC it names;
  - both are answered with the matching response;
  - **once a DLC is established, an MSC command for it goes out on DLC0 before any of its data** (27.010 §5.4.6.3.7, Codex plan r1).
- **A Test command** of up to N1 bytes gets back its whole verification pattern, during FCoff too (§5.4.6.3.4). The DLC0 queue holds a whole frame of N1 bytes.
- **PPP output:**
  - the callback returns the bytes it took: all of them, or 0 when DLC1's queue (4 KB) has no room, which counts `ppp_out_drop`;
  - a frame may span several calls;
  - nothing is promised beyond that.
- **PPP input:** `ERR_MEM` is retried 3 times, 1 ms apart, then the data is dropped and `ppp_in_drop` counts.
- **Recovery:**
  - no PPP address for 2 minutes: `AT+CFUN=1,1` over DLC2; AT silent: RECOVER;
  - RUNNING with no frame for 60 s and DLC2 silent: RECOVER;
  - RECOVER = CLD, 1 s, `RESET_N`, back to AT.
- **The plain-PPP fallback** when `AT+CMUX` is refused:
  - the signal is read before PPP only;
  - leaving data mode is `+++` with 1 s of silence on each side, then `ATH`; failing that, `RESET_N`.
- **Link priority** (`network.priority`, read at boot):
  - 0: Ethernet only. The modem is powered down with `AT+QPOWD=1` if it answers, and is never pulsed;
  - 1: Ethernet first;
  - 2: LTE first.

  The default route moves when the preferred link has no address (Ethernet: link down or no address), or no Internet while the other link has it (ruling 14).

  **The DNS is the chosen link's, at all times** (Codex plan r1, r2). PPP's `sdns()`/`cdns()` and DHCP would write lwIP's global servers whatever the route. So `netmgr` keeps each link's pair, taken from its own source (ruling 5), and writes the chosen link's pair again after each of these events:
  - a route change;
  - every PPP status callback, up or down;
  - every DHCP ACK, a renewal that keeps the same address included. A link that keeps its address but loses the Internet is found by the Internet check (ruling 14).
- **Before a gateway restart:** `AT+QPOWD=1` and up to 60 s for `POWERED DOWN`. PWRKEY is never pressed then.
- **Keep it simple** (the user): configs are read once at boot, run only the affected tests, TDD. Codex reviews before the PR.

## Review Focus

1. **A 2.5 s PWRKEY pulse on an EC600G-EU that is already on:** it must stay on (ruling 13). The firmware never sends a pulse of 3 s or more, on any path, including retries and the restart shutdown.
   - `test_lte_power` checks every path and every pulse length.
   - The bench checks the 2.5 s pulse on a running module (spec §7.2: "Bench phải xác nhận").
2. **DLC1's queue full in the middle of a PPP frame,** when the second or a later callback call of that frame finds no room:
   - the callback returns 0 and `ppp_out_drop` counts;
   - the modem side drops the cut frame by its FCS, and the next frame arrives whole.

   `test_cmux` checks this with a simulated PPP receiver.
3. **FCoff while PPP sends:** DLC1 holds its data (up to 4 KB), and DLC0 still answers. FCon resumes, in order. `test_cmux`.
4. **Ethernet unplugged under priority 1, then plugged back:**
   - the route moves to LTE and back;
   - the MQTT and bridge connections bound to the old link are closed, and reconnect over the new one;
   - the web UI stays reachable on Ethernet once the link is back.

   The bench case `priority` uses the PHY power-down (`L`).
5. **Continuous PPP load while DLC2 polls the signal:** `overrun` stays 0, and the signal keeps updating. The bench case `load`.

## Rulings to approve

1. **One owner of UART3 TX: `cmux_rx`.**
   - The other writers (the tcpip thread through PPP output, the `lte` task through DLC2 or plain AT) only queue.
   - `cmux_rx` drains the queues, through `cmux_tx_next` in CMUX mode or the plain ring otherwise, and sends by DMA.
   - The CMUX state is shared, so one mutex is held around every `cmux_*` call. It is never held across a UART wait.
2. **`cmux.c` is pull-based for TX.** `cmux_tx_next(m, out, max)` returns the next whole frame in this order: control frames (DLC0) first, then the data DLCs that are not stopped, round-robin, each frame carrying at most N1 bytes. This makes FCoff, MSC and the queues testable without a UART.
3. **Ring and queue sizes:**

   | Buffer | Size | Region |
   |---|---|---|
   | UART3 RX ring | 4 KB | SRAM1 (DMA) |
   | TX bounce | 2112 B | SRAM1 (DMA) |
   | CMUX RX frame buffer | 2112 B | AXI |
   | DLC1 TX queue | 4 KB | AXI |
   | DLC2 TX queue | 512 B | AXI |
   | DLC0 control queue (a Test response of N1 bytes fits) | 2112 B | AXI |
   | AT stream buffer (bytes received for the `lte` task) | 1 KB | AXI |

   The stacks are in DTCM (2 KB for `cmux_rx`, 6 KB for `lte`). `check_elf` gates the regions.
4. **The dial:**
   - before CMUX, on the plain UART: `ATE0`, `AT+CGSN` (IMEI), `AT+QCCID`, `AT+QGMR` (logged), and `AT+CGDCONT=1,"IP","<apn>"` (`internet` when the APN is empty, as on the ESP32);
   - then `AT+CMUX=…`, the DLCs, and `ATD*99#` on DLC1, waiting for `CONNECT`;
   - then PPPoS.
   - `apnAuth`: 0 none, 1 PAP, 2 CHAP, 3 both (lwIP's `ppp_set_auth` takes a mask).
5. **The DNS per link** (Codex plan r1).
   - **Ethernet:**
     - manual (`dnsMode` 1, or a static address): `dns1` and `dns2`;
     - automatic: the lease's servers. The existing `dhcp.c` patch hook becomes `LWIP_HOOK_DHCP_DNS(netif, n, servers)`:
       - it is called **once per accepted ACK**, with the whole list: 0, 1 or 2 servers, so a renewal with fewer servers clears the old ones;
       - DHCP never writes lwIP's global servers;
       - `netmgr` keeps the pair, then calls `dns_apply()`.
   - **The cell:**
     - `cellDnsMode` 1: `cellDns1` and `cellDns2`;
     - 0: the servers PPP negotiated (`ppp_set_usepeerdns(1)`, so IPCP asks for them).
     - The PPP status callback reads them from the negotiation result (`pcb->ipcp_gotoptions.dnsaddr[0..1]`, 0 for none), never from lwIP's globals. A PPP link without DNS gives an empty pair (Codex plan r2).
   - **`netcfg_dns(route, eth, cell, out[2])`** is pure and picks the pair.
   - **`dns_apply()`** writes that pair to lwIP's two servers, 0 included. It runs in the tcpip thread:
     - after a route change;
     - after every PPP status callback, up and down. On the way down, PPP's `cdns()` has already removed its servers from the globals, even when they equal Ethernet's, so the chosen link's pair is written again;
     - after every DHCP ACK.
6. **Closing connections on a route change:** in the tcpip thread, every active TCP PCB whose local address is not the new default netif's is aborted (`tcp_abort`). Their sockets then fail, and the bridge, MQTT, Edge Modbus TCP and TLS loops dial again, as they already do after an error. One place, no service changes. Listening PCBs and connections on the new link are untouched.
7. **`netmgr_online()`** is true when either link has an address.
8. **`/api/status`:**
   - `cell.state`: `disconnected` (priority 0, or no PPP), `connecting` (the `lte` task working) or `connected` (PPP has an address);
   - `cell.ip`, `cell.dns1`/`dns2`, `signal` (CSQ, 99 unknown), `netType` (`LTE` or the access technology as reported), `iccid`;
   - `system.imei`;
   - new: `cell.modem` (`ON`/`OFF`/`UNKNOWN`), `cell.mux` (the CMUX state or `PLAIN`), `cell.counters` (`overrun`, `badFcs`, `pppInDrop`, `pppOutDrop`).
9. **Heartbeats and registration** get IMEI (`sysmgr_payload` 1) and ICCID (3) from `lte_info()`. They stay empty until the modem has answered.
10. **The restart shutdown** runs in `sysmgr`'s restart task:
    - `lte_shutdown(60000)` is called before `NVIC_SystemReset` when the `lte` task runs and the module is ON;
    - it returns at once otherwise;
    - a factory reset or an OTA restart goes the same way.
11. **lwIP PPP is vendored** from the same tarball as lwIP (`STABLE-2_2_1_RELEASE`, SHA-256 `ce0b7461…539`): `src/netif/ppp/*.c` and `polarssl/md5.c`, with no patch. The README's "Left out" line is updated.
12. **The bench needs** the EC600G-EU, and later a data SIM and the Internet over LTE. A public broker carries the load (test.mosquitto.org, port 8883 with its CA), since the gateway behind CGNAT cannot be reached from outside. Without a SIM, which is the case now, the bench runs everything the module can do alone:
- the power paths and the 2.5 s pulse;
- AT, IMEI and the module firmware;
- `NO SIM`;
- CMUX open, MSC, DLC2 polling, and a DISC recovery.

PPP, `load`, `priority` over LTE, CFUN and `fallback` wait for a SIM. They are recorded as not run, and done in a follow-up when the SIM is fitted.
13. **The EC600G-EU takes the EC600U's timings** (the user). The bench checks each one on the module:
    - the time from release to AT, after PWRKEY and after `RESET_N`;
    - a 2.5 s PWRKEY on a running module leaves it on;
    - `POWERED DOWN` after `AT+QPOWD=1`;
    - `AT+CMUX` and the DLCs.

    If one fails, the value is changed in `lte_power.h` (one place), and spec §7.2 records it.
14. **An Internet check per link** (the user, 2026-10-16, after the bench: its Ethernet LAN keeps its address with no Internet, and priority 1 stayed on it). This replaces spec §7.2's "not detected" limit.
    - **The probe:**
      - an lwIP timer in the tcpip thread, every 30 s, with no new task;
      - it sends an ICMP echo to 8.8.8.8 and to 1.1.1.1 (fixed, not configurable) on each link that has an address, through that link: `raw_sendto_if_src`, the link's netif and its own address;
      - a link has Internet when either target answers within 2 s.
    - **The verdict** (pure: `inet_t`, `inet_result`): 3 rounds in a row without a reply (about 90 s) mean no Internet, and one reply means Internet again. A link is taken to have Internet until it fails.
    - **The route** (pure: `netcfg_pick_inet(priority, eth_ok, eth_inet, cell_ok, cell_inet)`):
      - the preferred link is left only when it has no Internet **and** the other link has both an address and Internet;
      - when both fail (ICMP blocked, say), the choice is by address alone, as before;
      - priority 0 stays on Ethernet.
    - **The cost:** about 100 bytes every 30 s on each link, about 12 KB an hour on LTE.
    - **`/api/status`:** `eth.internet` and `cell.internet` (booleans).
    - A route change through this check aborts the old link's connections, as ruling 6 says.

## Architecture

### `components/proto/cmux.{h,c}` (host-tested: `test_cmux`)

```c
#define CMUX_N1 2048
#define CMUX_FRAME_MAX (CMUX_N1 + 7)
typedef enum { CMUX_CLOSED, CMUX_OPEN_CTRL, CMUX_OPEN_DLC, CMUX_RUNNING, CMUX_FAILED } cmux_state_t;
typedef enum { CMUX_EV_RUNNING, CMUX_EV_DLC_CLOSED, CMUX_EV_FAILED } cmux_event_t;
typedef struct {
    void (*data)(void *ctx, int dlc, const uint8_t *p, size_t n);  // UIH payload on DLC1 or DLC2
    void (*event)(void *ctx, cmux_event_t ev, int dlc);
    void *ctx;
} cmux_io_t;
typedef struct { uint32_t bad_fcs, resyncs, out_drop, nsc; } cmux_counters_t;
typedef struct cmux cmux_t;  // the caller provides the storage: sizeof(cmux_t) from the header
void cmux_init(cmux_t *m, const cmux_io_t *io);
void cmux_start(cmux_t *m, uint32_t now_ms);                  // SABM DLC0 -> OPEN_CTRL
void cmux_input(cmux_t *m, const uint8_t *p, size_t n, uint32_t now_ms);
void cmux_tick(cmux_t *m, uint32_t now_ms);                    // T1 and N2
size_t cmux_send(cmux_t *m, int dlc, const uint8_t *p, size_t n);  // n or 0: all or nothing
size_t cmux_tx_next(cmux_t *m, uint8_t *out, size_t max);      // the next whole frame, 0 for none
void cmux_close(cmux_t *m);                                    // CLD on DLC0
cmux_state_t cmux_state(const cmux_t *m);
uint32_t cmux_last_rx_ms(const cmux_t *m);                      // for "no frame for 60 s"
const cmux_counters_t *cmux_counters(const cmux_t *m);
size_t cmux_frame(uint8_t *out, int dlc, uint8_t control, bool cr, const uint8_t *info, size_t n);  // encoder, tests too
```
- **FCS:** 27.010 Annex B. CRC-8, reflected polynomial 0xE0, init 0xFF, over the address, control and length octets only. With subset 0, only UIH frames carry information, and 27.010 §5.2.1.6 leaves it out of the FCS; SABM, UA, DM and DISC carry none. The result is `0xFF - crc`. A received frame checks to `0xCF`.
- **The parser** syncs on `0xF9`. A wrong FCS or a length over N1 drops the frame and counts it; it then resyncs at the next `0xF9`. Frames may be cut anywhere between `cmux_input` calls.
- **Control on DLC0** (UIH, type octets):
  - FCon `0xA1`/`0xA3`, FCoff `0x61`/`0x63`;
  - MSC `0xE1`/`0xE3`, with the DLC in its address octet and FC as bit 1 of the V.24 octet;
  - Test `0x21`/`0x23`;
  - CLD `0xC1`/`0xC3`;
  - anything else gets NSC `0x11`.
- **DM or DISC on an open DLC** sends `CMUX_EV_DLC_CLOSED`.
- **SABM without UA within T1** is sent again, up to N2 times, then `CMUX_FAILED` and `CMUX_EV_FAILED`.
- **After the UA for DLC1 and for DLC2,** an MSC command for that DLC (RTC, RTR and DV set, FC clear) is queued on DLC0. `cmux_tx_next` sends control first, so it precedes the DLC's data. RUNNING follows the second MSC.
- **A Test command's response** is built in the DLC0 queue (2112 B), with the whole pattern.

## Measurements (T6, the second bench board)

**Module and SIM:**
- EC600G-EU, firmware `EC600GEULDR01A06M04_01.001.01.001`.
- A VinaPhone SIM: LTE, CSQ 19 to 25. PPP gets a 10.x address, with DNS from IPCP.

**Timings:**

| Step | Measured |
|---|---|
| `RDY` after PWRKEY 2.5 s | about 8 s |
| `AT` after `RESET_N` 200 ms | 1.0 s |
| `AT` after the release of PWRKEY | at once |
| `POWERED DOWN` after `AT+QPOWD=1` | 0.45 s |
| CMUX running after the identity | 150 ms |
| PPP up after `CONNECT` | about 6 s |

- The ready deadline is set to 10 s (ruling 13: twice the measured time, but not under 10 s).
- A 2.5 s PWRKEY on a running module leaves it on, and PPP keeps its address.

**`lte_check.py`:**
- **identity:** IMEI, ICCID, and the IMEI heartbeat on UART1's TCP client.
- **power:** a restart gets QPOWD, then the full power-on sequence at the next boot.
- **mux:** a DISC on DLC2 recovers, with PPP up again in 34 s.
- **priority:**
  - priority 2: the route goes over LTE, MQTT reaches broker.emqx.io, and NTP sets the clock;
  - priority 1 on the bench LAN, which has no Internet: the Internet check moves the route to LTE;
  - the PHY down 30 s and back.
- **load:** 1 090 000 bytes down over LTE in 5 minutes, all of them reaching UART1.
  - overrun 0, bad FCS 0, `pppInDrop` and `pppOutDrop` 0;
  - the signal read on DLC2 throughout.
- **Stack left:** `cmux_rx` 1 608 of 2 048 B, `lte` 5 248 of 6 144 B. The lowest heap was 334 776 B.

**Not run:**
- **The up direction of `load`, and the regressions that need UART1 and UART2 wired to each other** (`mqtt_check publish`, `bridge_check tcpclient`, `edge_check basic`). This board has no RS485 loop: UART2 sent 6 bytes and UART1 got none.
- **The plain-PPP fallback:** the module accepts `AT+CMUX`, so the fallback path cannot be reached on this board.

**Bench findings (firmware):**
1. **The LTE pins were off by one** (fixed): PWRKEY is on PA6 and RESET_N on PA5, as the schematic draws them. They had been read from the PDF's extracted text.
2. **CMUX's 2-octet length field** (fixed): the second octet is L8..L15, with no EA bit (27.010 §5.2.1.5). The encoder and the decoder were wrong in the same way, so `test_cmux` passed while the modem's frames over 127 bytes failed their FCS.
3. **The first SIM did not register** (`+CEREG: 0,2`). The firmware now dials only when the module is registered.
4. **A bad APN cannot cause "no address" on this network:** LTE attaches with its default bearer. The CFUN case uses the bench toggle `q` instead.
5. **The bench LAN keeps its address with no Internet.** This led to ruling 14, the Internet check.
